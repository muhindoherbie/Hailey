
## What's new in 3.1

### Free AI models, chosen for you

- **Only free services, best first:** Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, NVIDIA, Z.ai (GLM), Ollama Cloud, and Ollama on your PC. Claude, which is paid, is no longer offered (an existing Claude setup keeps working).
- **A model dropdown for each service**, with its top free models in priority order: for example Gemini 3.8 Flash, GPT-OSS 120B and Qwen 3.8 on Groq, `openrouter/free` (auto) and Gemma 4 on OpenRouter, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on NVIDIA and Ollama Cloud. DeepSeek's own API has no free tier, so DeepSeek is offered through services that serve it free.
- **Check my key** asks the service which models your key can use, marks each one, and moves to the best one that works. For OpenRouter it also shows any other free models available right now; for Ollama on your PC it lists the models you have installed.
- **Automatic switching:** if a model is retired (404), busy (503) or over its free limit (429), Hailey moves on to the next free model in the list by itself, and the error says which models it tried.

These fixes come from the errors seen in setup: Groq removed Llama from its free tier, Google moved new keys to Gemini 3.x, and Gemini was briefly overloaded.

### A smoother, more polished look (Material 3)

- **Every tap answers back.** Buttons, cards, chips, tabs and menu items show a soft press tint and a slight press-in, so you can feel what you touched.
- **Smooth page changes.** Moving between screens uses a quick fade, the navigation bar's pill grows into place, and dialogs slide up from the bottom as sheets with a handle.
- **Cleaner screens.** To-dos keeps search and a **Filter** button on one row (project, priority and sort open under it and show how many are on). In Settings, each field sits full-width under its label. The top bar tints only once you scroll.
- **Material 3 controls:** larger switches, tabs with a rounded indicator, crisp field outlines, and disabled buttons that look "not ready yet" rather than broken.
- **Easier to read.** Every text colour is checked against WCAG contrast in all four themes (using Adobe Leonardo); warning text was darkened to pass.
- **Still fast.** All motion is plain CSS and native browser View Transitions, with no animation library. Long lists draw only what is on screen, and everything respects "Reduce motion".

### Fixes found in testing

- **Changes are saved straight away.** Closing the app within a second or two of a change could lose it; the phone app now saves within a fraction of a second.
- **Word and PowerPoint files work on the phone.** Asking Hailey for a `.docx` or `.pptx` file failed with "internal error" in the Android app; it now makes them like the PC version does.

### Tested before release

Every feature was driven through the app's own screens in a phone-sized browser (36 checks), and the real APK is now tested on an Android emulator on every change: setup, streaming chat, to-dos, a notification, a reminder ringing while the app is closed, data kept after a force-stop, sharing into the app, saving to Downloads, text-to-speech, the back button and the widget.
