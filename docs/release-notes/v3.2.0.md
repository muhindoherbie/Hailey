## What's new in 3.2

### Test speed: find the fastest free AI model from your phone

- In **Settings → AI service**, tap **Test speed**. Hailey sends a short message to each free model your key can use, times how long each takes to start answering and to finish, lists them fastest first, and picks the fastest one that works. Tap **Save and test** to keep it.
- Timing happens from your phone, with your own key, so it reflects your connection and the time of day. If the key is rejected, it stops after the first try and tells you.
- In published benchmarks (October 2026), GPT-OSS 120B on **Groq** answers about three times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash. Gemini is still the one to use for photos.

### Fixes found in deeper testing

- **Stop keeps what you've read.** Tapping Stop during a reply used to throw away the words already shown. Now they stay, marked "You stopped this reply".
- **Long messages no longer hide the Send button** under the bottom bar.
- **To-dos with more than 500 items** now say "Showing 500 of 1,240" and have a **Show more** button. Before, the list silently stopped at 500.
- **No more "Too many requests" on the phone.** The phone app no longer limits itself when you do a lot at once.
- **Easier to read and use with a screen reader:**
  - Grey hint text is darker, at 4.5:1 contrast everywhere.
  - Icon-only buttons have names for screen readers.
  - Links inside sentences are underlined.
  - Progress bars and wide tables are labelled.
  - Every screen now passes an automatic WCAG 2.2 AA check (axe-core) in light and dark.
- **Phone-friendly wording.** With no AI service set up, chat and research now point you to Settings rather than to server files. The calendar-feed option, which needs a server, is hidden on the phone.
- **AI settings fit small phones** (320 px wide) even with a long usage history.
- **A screen that can't load shows "Try again"** instead of a blank page.
