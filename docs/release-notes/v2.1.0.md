### What's new in Hailey 2.1

Hailey 2.1 is a big step towards being a companion that works for you on its own, in your other apps and by voice — using free options wherever a paid service would otherwise be needed.

**Talk to Hailey anywhere**
- **Voice mode:** tap the speaker in any chat (or Hailey's face on Home) for a hands-free conversation — Hailey listens, answers out loud and listens again. It uses your browser's own speech service, so it costs nothing extra.
- **Telegram (free):** create your own bot with @BotFather, paste its token on the new **Messaging** page, and send the 6-digit code. Message Hailey, send voice notes and photos, and approve actions with buttons. Add Hailey to a family or team group: it answers only when mentioned, keeps your private data out of the group, and `/stop` makes it go quiet.
- **WhatsApp:** connect a WhatsApp Business Cloud API number (free for conversations you start). Needs Hailey on a public HTTPS address.
- **Voice notes** are transcribed with any OpenAI-compatible speech-to-text service — Groq's free tier or a local Whisper server (`TRANSCRIBE_BASE_URL`).

**Hailey works while you don't**
- **Routines:** your own scheduled instructions — "every Friday log my wins", "check this price every morning", "a five-minute Luganda lesson each weekday". 18 ready-made ideas, or ask in chat. Results arrive as a notification; anything that needs your OK waits for you.
- **Ramble it out:** talk or type everything on your mind; Hailey sorts it into tasks, reminders and notes, and you pick what to keep.

**Connect your apps**
- **Connected apps (MCP):** link email, calendar, notes, project tools and thousands of other apps. Sign in directly with apps such as Notion and Linear, or use a free connector service (Zapier, Composio, Pipedream) for Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook and more. Hailey reads freely; sending, changing and deleting ask first.

**More of what you need**
- **Discover:** 35 ideas to try — set a goal, plan meals, find unused subscriptions, chase invoices, a bedtime story, fix something at home, prepare a phone call, find a place nearby, plan a trip.
- **Photos and video:** attach photos (shrunk on your phone), or a video — Hailey looks at still frames from it. Needs a model that can see images, such as Claude.
- **Share to Hailey** from any app's Share menu, and share Hailey's replies to WhatsApp or anywhere else.
- **Find places** with OpenStreetMap (free), and **Add my location** in chat.
- **Prepare a call:** a short script saved as a note with a tap-to-dial link.

**Make it yours**
- A new **first-run setup**: your name, a name for your assistant, what you want help with and your first routines.
- **Rename your assistant**, choose a **reply language**, and let Hailey **learn your writing style** from a few samples.
- **Specialist modes** — Chef, Coach and Tutor — unlocked by using Hailey; a **weekly streak** and **Your week in numbers** on Home; an animated Hailey.
- **Rate replies** 👍 👎, and **clear all** notifications.
- **Android:** a home-screen widget (Ramble, Talk, New chat), Hailey in the Share menu, and new app shortcuts.

**Also**
- The upload limit is now **100 MB**, and very large documents index in seconds instead of minutes.

**Upgrading:** your data is kept. The database update only adds new tables and columns and runs automatically on first start. You'll see the new first-run setup once — skip it if you like. New optional server settings are listed in `.env.example` (`TRANSCRIBE_*`, `MCP_ALLOW_PRIVATE_NETWORK`, `NOMINATIM_URL`, `OPENAI_COMPAT_VISION`).
