### What's new in Hailey 2.2

A friendlier, chat-first Hailey, laid out the way companion apps work best on a phone, and tuned for both Android and PC.

**A new home**
- **Chat-first Home:** your assistant front and centre — tap it to talk hands-free — with one "What's on your mind?" box (type, speak, or tap the speaker for a voice conversation) and quick starts: *Plan my day*, *Ramble it out*, *Remind me…*, *Just talk*, *Ideas*.
- **Highlight cards** you swipe through: today's to-dos (and anything overdue), your briefing, what's coming up next, your week and streak, and an idea to try. Your full dashboard is still below, under *Your day*.
- **Recent chats** right on Home.

**Simpler navigation**
- On phones, five tabs: **Home · Chats · Discover · Your stuff · Me**. On PCs, the same sections lead the sidebar.
- **Your stuff:** one place for everything your assistant holds — tasks and reminders, routines, notes and documents, projects, memories, connected apps and messaging — with live counts and a heads-up when something waits for your approval.
- **Me:** your profile, streak and achievements, notifications, approvals, messaging, connected apps and settings.

**Dress up your assistant**
- Your assistant can now wear accessories: a **crown** after a 4-week streak, a **chef's hat**, **graduation cap** or **sports headband** with Chef, Tutor and Coach modes, and a **party hat** any time. Choose on the *Me* page, or leave it on *Automatic*.
- A brighter, friendlier look for the mascot, in light and dark mode.

**Made for Android and PC**
- Softer, rounder design with pill buttons and gradient cards.
- On touch screens: larger tap targets, 16px fields so Android doesn't zoom in, and the tab bar steps aside while the keyboard is open so the message box stays visible. Screen heights follow Android's moving address bar.
- On PCs: a wider layout on big screens, hover states and clear keyboard focus.

**Upgrading:** your data is kept; there are no database changes in this release.
